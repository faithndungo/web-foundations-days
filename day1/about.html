About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool designed to help you capture your thoughts and ideas quickly. Whether it's a sudden burst of inspiration or a daily to-do list, QuickNotes ensures nothing gets lost.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes application in your browser.
  2. Type your thought into the "New note" input field.
  3. Click the "Add Note" button to save it to your list.

Features

  • Instantly capture text notes.
  • Simple and intuitive user interface.
  • Organize your tasks efficiently.
